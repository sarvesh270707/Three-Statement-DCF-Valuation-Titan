### Scenario Descriptions

#### 1. Bear Case
- **Macro Volatility & Gold Price Spikes**: Unprecedented spikes in international gold prices cause consumer demand deferrals.
- **Heightened Competition**: Regional organized retail players (Kalyan, Malabar, Joyalukkas) offer aggressive making-charge discounts, compressing gross margins to 20.5%.
- **Valuation Impact**: Implied share price of **₹2,451.63 - ₹2,827.36**.

#### 2. Base Case (Management Consensus)
- **Continued Formalization**: Titan continues gaining market share from unorganized independent jewelers.
- **Omnichannel Synergy**: Caratlane and Mia capture the younger, lightweight, daily-wear fine jewellery segment.
- **Store Expansion**: Net addition of 150-200 stores annually across India and international diaspora hubs.
- **Valuation Impact**: Implied share price of **₹3,257.67** (at 35x EBITDA).

#### 3. Bull Case (Accelerated Luxury Growth)
- **Studded Share Expansion**: Studded jewellery ratio surpasses 35%, expanding gross margins to 24.5% and EBITDA margin to 13.5%.
- **Global Scaling**: High-margin international expansion in Dubai, Singapore, and the US scales significantly.
- **Valuation Impact**: Implied share price of **₹3,944.58 - ₹4,439.26** (at 42x EBITDA).
